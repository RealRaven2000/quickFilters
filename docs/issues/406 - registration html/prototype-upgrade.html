quickFilters helps you create and manage Thunderbird filters. quickFilters licenses fund ongoing development, new features, and compatibility updates. Each license lasts one year, helping support the work needed to keep pace with Thunderbird.

One year at a time

A license covers one year. Renew an expired license or extend a current one to continue with your chosen edition. Learn more…

Pro licenses
A new license runs for one year from payment. When renewing or extending Pro, you receive a full year from payment or one year beyond your existing expiry date, whichever gives you more time.

Pro → Domain upgrades
The shop uses your existing license to determine upgrade eligibility and terms.

Domain licenses
Domain licenses are reviewed and issued manually. Renewal adds one year to your existing expiry date, even if the license has already expired. This encourages timely renewal; the final term is confirmed during review.

Existing license
Your current license is passed to the shop so the new expiry date can be calculated.

Renewal and upgrade benefits depend on license information passed to the shop. Switching products in the shop or security software removing that information can prevent the shop from applying your existing entitlement.

Licenses do not renew automatically. quickFilters will remind you before your license expires.

Your email, your license

A personal license is tied to your email address. You can use it on multiple Thunderbird installations when the licensed address is configured as the default identity of an account in each installation. The licensed features also work with all your other email accounts in Thunderbird. No separate licenses needed.

A Domain license covers users with matching addresses under the licensed domain.
