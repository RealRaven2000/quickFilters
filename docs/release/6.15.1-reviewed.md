## Proposed response and privacy policy draft

### Experiment API finding

`getProFeatureUsage()` is not needed. It only returned a local snapshot of trial counters, and the code has no callers. It has been removed from the Utilities Experiment schema and implementation. `consumeProFeatureUsage()` remains because it records/enforces trial use locally.

### Privacy policy text for ATN

quickFilters does not collect or transmit personal information for analytics, advertising, or tracking. Add-on settings and premium-feature trial counters are stored locally in the Thunderbird profile and are not sent to the developer. Trial counters record use counts for premium features; backup/restore trial records also store a local session identifier and the local calendar date to enforce trial limits. Your email messages, filters, account credentials, and address-book data are processed locally to provide the add-on's features and are not sent to the developer.

When you choose to order or renew a license, quickFilters opens its registration page. If you continue to checkout, the registration page sends the name and email address you enter to FastSpring, our third-party checkout provider, to process the order and issue a license. If you start checkout from a premium-feature prompt, quickFilters may pass the name of that feature to FastSpring as an order referrer so the order can identify what led you to checkout. For renewals, the existing license key may be sent to FastSpring as the order referrer. FastSpring processes payment and order information under its own privacy policy. quickFilters uses the email address associated with your Thunderbird account locally to check whether an installed license matches that address; the add-on does not transmit your Thunderbird account email for this check.

The add-on's websites and checkout are opened over HTTPS. The developer's website may receive standard connection data from your browser when you visit it, and FastSpring receives the information you submit during checkout, under their respective privacy practices. quickFilters itself has no analytics or telemetry service.
